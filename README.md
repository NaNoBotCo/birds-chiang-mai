# Birds of Chiang Mai · นกเชียงใหม่

https://nanobotco.github.io/birds-chiang-mai/ · Thai: https://nanobotco.github.io/birds-chiang-mai/th/

- `docs/bird.js` draws every bird from its parameters in `tools/birds.json`.
- `docs/sky.js` computes the sun (NOAA), the moon (Meeus) and the Thai lunar day (wan phra table, BE 2569).
- `docs/app.js` runs the sky scene, the Coucal Clock, the cards, the chorus wheel, the mountain, the legends and the spectrogram dock.
- `tools/build.py` writes both language pages from `tools/birds.json`, `tools/credits.json` and the copy inside it.

Test hooks: `?t=<minutes after midnight>` fixes the sky's time; `?card&t=1098` at 1200×630 is the share card.
